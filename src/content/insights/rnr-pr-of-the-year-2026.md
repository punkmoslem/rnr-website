**JAKARTA, 18 August 2026** — R&R Communications has been named **PR Practitioner of the Year 2026** in the **PR Agency** category at the **Indonesia PR of the Year** awards, chosen by journalist vote. The award was presented by **MIX Marketing & Communication** and **SWA Media Group** at LSPR Campus B Jakarta on Tuesday, 18 August 2026, signed by Kemal E. Gani, Chairman of SWA Media Group.

This is the second time R&R has received the honour — the first was in 2018, when the agency was named PR Agency of the Year. The recognition reflects a consistent track record in integrated communications strategy, media relations, and reputation management for both multinational and Indonesian brands across industries.

The award follows R&R's **Best Media Relations Strategy** win at the **PR Awards 2026** earlier this year for its work with Bayer Indonesia — making 2026 a double-award year for the agency.

Founded in 2000 as Rika dan Rekan, R&R Communications has managed over 2,000 projects for 150+ brands, earning approximately 100,000 media placements over 26 years. Current clients include MSD Indonesia, Bayer, OPPO, ASUS, Paragon Corp, and PZ Cussons.
