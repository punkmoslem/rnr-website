**JAKARTA, 11 May 2026** — R&R Communications has been named winner of **Best Media Relations Strategy** at the **Marketing-Interactive PR Awards 2026**, for its work with **Bayer Indonesia**.

The award recognises a media relations approach built on substance over noise: earning coverage that carries weight because it deserves to, and sustaining the kind of newsroom relationships that turn a client's story into news the public trusts. It is the discipline R&R has practised since 2000 — sitting between brands and the people they serve, and making the connection hold.

The recognition caps a multi-year partnership with Bayer Indonesia spanning product launches, community and education programmes, plant visits, and the ongoing "The Science Behind" series — work that consistently placed Bayer's science and purpose into national conversation.

The PR Awards, organised by Marketing-Interactive, are among the region's most competitive honours in public relations and communications.
