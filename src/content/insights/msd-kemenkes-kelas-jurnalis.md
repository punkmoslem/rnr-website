**JAKARTA, 22 April 2026** — MSD Indonesia, together with the Ministry of Health of the Republic of Indonesia, convened Kelas Jurnalis 2026, a journalism class dedicated to deepening media understanding of HPV (Human Papillomavirus) prevention in boys — a critical step in broadening national immunization awareness beyond cervical cancer prevention in girls.

The program brought national health journalists together with clinicians and Ministry of Health officials to examine the epidemiology of HPV-related diseases and the role of accurate reporting in public health outcomes.

Kelas Jurnalis continues the education-first partnership between MSD Indonesia and R&R Communications that began in 2022, which has produced the "Tenang untuk Menang" campaign and journalism roadshows reaching key cities across Indonesia.
