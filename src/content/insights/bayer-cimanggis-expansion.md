**DEPOK, 15 April 2026** — Bayer Indonesia today inaugurated the expansion of its Cimanggis production facility, a Rp 99 billion investment that increases production capability for MMS (Multivitamin and Mineral Supplements) lines and strengthens research and development capacity.

The expansion supports the national health ecosystem agenda — increasing domestic production of consumer health products, deepening the local talent base, and positioning Indonesian manufacturing within Bayer's global supply chain.

The announcement continues an eight-year strategic communication partnership between Bayer Indonesia and R&R Communications, spanning product launches, community programs, plant visits, and the ongoing "The Science Behind" education series.
